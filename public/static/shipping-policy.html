Shipping Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2026

Order Processing

All orders are processed within 1–2 business days from our Mumbai warehouse. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays will be processed on the next business day.

Shipping Timelines

  • Metro cities: 2–4 business days
  • Tier-2 & Tier-3 cities: 4–6 business days
  • Remote locations: 6–9 business days

Shipping Charges

We offer complimentary shipping on all orders above ₹2,999. For orders below this amount, a flat shipping fee of ₹199 applies.

Order Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a tracking link via email and SMS. You can also track your order anytime from the Track Order page.

International Shipping

Currently, we ship only within India. International shipping will be available soon.

Address Changes

If you need to change your shipping address, please contact us within 12 hours of placing your order at hello@houseofviraasat.com.
