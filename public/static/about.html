Our Story

Crafted with Viraasat.

A house built on the belief that heritage deserves a place in every modern wardrobe.

Born from tradition.

House of Viraasat began with a simple idea — that the richness of Indian craft deserves a place in contemporary wardrobes. Every piece we create is a bridge between the artisan's hand and the modern wearer.

We work closely with weavers, embroiderers, and tailors across India. From the first sketch to the final stitch, our process is slow, intentional, and rooted in respect for the craft.

Made for celebrations.

Whether it's a wedding, a festival, or a quiet family gathering — our collections are designed to make every moment feel worthy of memory.

We believe that the clothes you wear to your most important moments should carry meaning. That's why every Viraasat piece is finished with hand detailing, chosen fabrics, and an attention to comfort that lets you be present.

Responsibly made.

We believe luxury should not come at the cost of people or planet. Our production processes are designed to minimize waste, support fair wages, and celebrate the slow craft of Indian textiles.

Every order you place helps sustain a weaver's livelihood and keeps an ancient craft alive for the next generation.
