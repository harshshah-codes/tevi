Return & Refund

Last updated: 1 January 2026

Return Window

We offer a 7-day return and exchange window from the date of delivery. To be eligible for a return, items must be:

  • Unworn, unwashed, and unused
  • In their original packaging with all tags intact
  • Accompanied by the original invoice

Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

  • Customized or made-to-order pieces
  • Items marked as "Final Sale" or "Non-returnable"
  • Innerwear, accessories, and personal care items
  • Items damaged due to misuse or improper care

How to Initiate a Return

To initiate a return, please contact us at hello@houseofviraasat.com with your order number and reason for return. Our team will guide you through the process.

Refund Process

Once your return is received and inspected, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. If approved, refunds will be processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method.

Exchange Policy

Exchanges are subject to availability. If the requested size or colour is unavailable, we will offer a full refund or store credit.

Shipping Costs for Returns

Return shipping costs are borne by the customer, except in cases where the item received is damaged or incorrect.
