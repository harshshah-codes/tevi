ABOUT HOUSE OF VIRAASAT

House of Viraasat is a brand inspired by the traditional skills of local artisans spread across India. We celebrate Indian craft through thoughtfully curated ethnic wear, refined fabrics and timeless silhouettes.

Our collection brings together mature minimalism and effortless elegance — created for celebrations, special occasions and everyday moments that deserve a little more meaning.

We believe in sophisticated fashion, beautiful craftsmanship and the enduring charm of Indian heritage.
